## Monetization: Sponsor-First (replaces affiliate links)

**Why the pivot:** generic affiliate links (ClickBank/ShareASale) break the identity immersion and pay pennies per click. The blueprint's §8.2 model — **sponsored quests with real-world rewards** — pays a flat fee or CPA per verified action and fits the identity fantasy.

**The model:**
- A sponsor (local smoothie bar, bookstore, gym) pays a **flat monthly fee ($200-500+/mo)** for a themed quest.
- Users complete the quest → **real reward delivered automatically** (e.g. 20% discount code, 1 free smoothie) via a voucher platform webhook (Giftbit / Tremendous, free or cheap tiers).
- No in-app ad feel; the reward *is* the prize for their identity.

**MVP sequence (sponsor-first, do not build ahead of a signed sponsor):**
1. Sign sponsor #1 for a fixed fee.
2. Hardcode **one quest** for that sponsor (blueprint §8.2), with sponsor display on the challenge detail screen.
3. Automate reward delivery for that single sponsor via a voucher API webhook.
4. Launch → sponsor markets to their customers → sign sponsor #2 (bookstore/reading quest).

**Guardrails:**
- No affiliate URL opening, no reward card that leaves the app, no server-published feed for now (the generic catalog stands).
- Revenue requires at least one signed sponsor before any monetization code.

**Later (Tier 3):** quest marketplace (multi-sponsor), 1:1 coaching / habit-system design, The Emerge Method guide (also a near-term willingness-to-pay test).

## Strategy: GTM + Community-First (unchanged — still the growth engine)

- **Content-led organic GTM:** the app is a content factory (recap visuals, world growth time-lapses).
- **Community-first distribution:** engage high-intent communities (Reddit, FB Groups, WhatsApp/Telegram, Quora) at 10:1 value-to-mention before/while building owned channels.
- **Beachhead:** Nigeria/Africa (Paystack ₦), global plumbing in parallel.
- **Launch events:** Product Hunt (target ~Oct 1) + sponsor audience announcements.

**Funnel:** content → link-in-bio → app download → completion → shareable recap → UGC → next content. Sponsored quests plug into the funnel as the monetization moment.
