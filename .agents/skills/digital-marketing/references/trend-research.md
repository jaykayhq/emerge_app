# Trend Research Methodology

How to detect trends early, judge whether to ride them, and schedule content
against the calendar. Every trend decision feeds the platform playbooks.

## Where trends are found (before search catches up)

1. **In-app signals (earliest, near-zero lag).** Your For You feed, the
   platform search bar autocomplete, sound pages. Train a research account with
   20–30 min/day of deliberate niche watching (watch-to-completion only).
2. **TikTok Creative Center** (ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter) — the
   free official trends dashboard. Check the **Nigeria region + creator filter**,
   not global. 5 screens in <10 min: Trends→Hashtags (sort by **growth** not
   volume), Trends→Songs (Breakout filter), Trends→Creators, Trends→Videos;
   Inspiration→Top Ads for proven hooks. Lag 2–5 days.
3. **Paid creative scan.** The Top Ads library shows what advertisers scale
   (proof money follows). Lag 1–3 weeks.
4. **Off-platform context.** Google Trends (the "is it real" check),
   Exploding Topics, Reddit rising, annual reports (TikTok Trend Report,
   Pinterest Predicts, Instagram Trend Talk).

## Lifecycle (what trend type lasts how long)

By type (sources: webtonic.io/blog/tiktok-trend-discovery,
theshelf.com/the-blog/tiktok-trends-explained/):
- **Sounds** peak fastest — 5–14 days. If a sound passed **~500,000 videos,
  you're late** unless the angle is differentiated. Watch the **growth slope**,
  not total volume.
- **Formats** (edits/transitions/shot structures) last 3–8 weeks.
- **Topics/themes** run 1–6 months.
- **Challenges** 2–4 weeks.

The Shelf's framing: *Trend Moments* = flashy fireworks (2 days–3 weeks),
*Trend Signals* = steady drummers (3 weeks–2 months, calendar-anchored),
*Trend Forces* = long cultural shifts (2+ months).

## Hype vs real demand — 5-check test

1. Does Google Trends show a **search line** behind the social hype
   (search-backed = real) or is it social-only hype?
2. Is the curve **90-day sustained** or a single spike? (Exploding Topics
   distinguishes long-term trends "likely to grow throughout 2026" from fads.)
3. Has commercial content appeared (affiliate articles, reviews, sponsors)?
   Money follows validated demand.
4. Does it **fit the brand** — Emerge's identity pillar and "no shame" tone?
   (Forest: "a companion, not a coach — no shame, no preaching".) A trend that
   mocks failure is unfittable.
5. Can we **ship an asset in days** (production cost + brand-fit gate)?

**When to skip:** meme-of-the-week with no fit; anything that abandons the
identity narrative; trends already at plateau (riding at peak = being late);
anything requiring significant paid production on an unproven audience.

## Confirmation heuristic

Keep a creator watchlist of 15–25 accounts. **If 3 independent creators adopt
the same format within 48 hours, treat it as confirmed.** Log new search
autocomplete phrasings weekly; read repeated comments as content ideas.

## Score before you film (run weekly, ~30 min)

Score candidates /25 across: growth slope, format fit, brand safety, production
cost, shelf life. **Shoot only candidates scoring >17/25** (typically 2 of 6
logged make it).

**Speed is the edge.** Publish within **48–72 hours** of confirming
acceleration; slower than 3 days lands in the crowded middle.

## Seasonal calendar anchors for a habit app

- **New Year resolutions** — global Q1 peak (biggest).
- "New month, new me"; **back-to-school** (Sep — critical for Nigerian
  students); **exam seasons**; **Ramadan**; fitness/wellness peaks.
- Use trends.google.com/trends/calendars (official Google seasonal calendar) to
  plan months ahead.

## Tooling (2025–26)

TikTok Creative Center (free), Google Trends, Exploding Topics (free tier + Pro
forecasts + TikTok Insights add-on), Glimpse (Google Trends plugin for breakout
search terms), Pentos / Virlo / RivalIQ (paid deep analytics — only after
Proving retention). Both reports agree: **55% of Gen Z find products on TikTok
first**, and **UGC reads 2.4x more authentic than branded content** — use
trends via real users' world-growth clips and creator voices, not overt brand
posts (explodingtopics.com/blog/tiktok-trend-research-tools).

## Prompts

- "Check TikTok Creative Center top sounds/formats for Nigeria this week; rank
  the top 5 for fit with a habit-growth app."
- "Pull Google Trends for 'new year resolutions', 'discipline', 'glow up' —
  classify each as seasonal-spike, sustained, or breakout; recommend which to
  schedule for which month."
- "Score these 8 trend candidates /25 on my scorecard and recommend what to
  shoot this week (target >17/25)."
