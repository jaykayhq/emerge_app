# Copy-Paste Prompt Library

Reusable research + content prompts, adapted from the Marketing Strategy
Guidebook's ChatGPT methodology for Emerge (an identity-first habit app, not an
e-commerce store). Swap `[PLACEHOLDERS]` with real inputs. Prompts are grouped by
stage. "Act as an expert…" framing keeps outputs consistent.

## Product Details

- **Benefits & features:**
  "As a digital marketing expert, give a comprehensive breakdown of the key
  benefits and features in separate lists of [PRODUCT]. Explain how these
  features address customer pain points and what makes this product stand out
  from competitors. Highlight any testimonials, awards, or certifications that
  can be leveraged for credibility and trust."
- **Feature → benefit → emotion:** "From these 40 features, map each to a
  user-facing benefit and the emotion it triggers. Output a table:
  feature | benefit | emotion."
- **Positioning statement:** "Draft a positioning statement in the template
  'For [audience] who [need], [product] is a [category] that [benefit], unlike
  [alternative] which [gap].' Then 2 alternatives and diff them."

## Target-Audience Persona

- **Generate persona:**
  "Act as an expert digital marketer. Provide a comprehensive target-audience
  persona for selling [PRODUCT] to [NICHE]. Include: Demographics, Geographic
  location, Psychographics, Professional background, Pain points and
  challenges, Goals and aspirations, Shopping habits and preferences, Media
  consumption, Influencers and decision-makers, Brand perceptions and
  preferences. Assume no prior audience data; base it on general market trends
  and consumer behavior. At the end, suggest different niches to target."
- **Update persona with niche:**
  "Update the following target-audience persona and aim it towards [NICHE]."
- **Synthesis (Emerge):** "From these 8 interview transcripts + 40 Reddit
  quotes, cluster pain points into 3 behavioral segments, each with a hire
  metaphor." / "Rank candidate first-niches on felt pain, community
  reachability, sponsor appeal, content surface."

## Competitor Analysis

- **Reviews → pros/cons:**
  "Summarize the following reviews into lists of pros and cons. At the end give
  me suggestions on how I may improve based on this feedback."
- **Lists → strategy:**
  "Act as a digital marketing expert. Summarize this list into constructive
  feedback I can use for a digital marketing strategy."
- **Theme counting:** "Export 25 recent reviews per app → 3 praised themes with
  verbs, 3 complained themes with quotes, and theme counts."
- **Counter-position:** "The biggest pain users of our top competitor review
  about is ___, so we should ___."

## Market Research

- **TAM/SAM/SOM:** "Size TAM/SAM/SOM for a paid habit-tracker app in Nigeria
  bottom-up: smartphone users × % who install self-help apps × % who pay. Show
  every assumption and source. Then repeat top-down. Diff the two."
- **Demand:** "From Google Trends list topics related to 'habit tracker' and
  'self improvement' with rising/breakout status in Nigeria, last 12 months;
  flag seasonality."
- **Community mining:** "Search r/getdisciplined, r/productivity, r/Nigeria for
  the top recurring habit-app complaints; quote 10 threads with timestamps."

## SEO / Content (adapted from guidebook)

- **Article ideas:**
  "Act as an SEO expert. Generate 10 engaging blog article ideas related to the
  [NICHE] niche. Provide valuable insights, tips, or trends. Max title 60
  characters, 4 unique sections per title, all vaguely related to [PRODUCT].
  Optimize for the keyword [KEYWORD]."
- **Section text:**
  "Act as a blogger. Write engaging blog post paragraphs on the section
  [SECTION]. Use easy language, max sentence length 20 words, at least 650
  words, light-hearted tone, you-sentences, minimum 3 subheads. Optimize toward
  [KEYWORD] without overdoing it."
- **Meta description:**
  "Act as an SEO expert. Write an SEO-optimized meta description for the
  following article, max 155 characters, optimized for [KEYWORD]."
- **Backlink outreach email:**
  "Act as an outreach specialist. Summarize the following article in max 8
  sentences, designed as an email asking for backlinks in a relevant niche.
  Friendly everyday tone, highlight the benefits for their readers, clear
  call-to-action, express gratitude."
- **Refer a keyword-intent list:** map "curiosity intent" (what is, how to,
  meaning, definition, guide, examples, ideas, best, top, trends, alternatives)
  to blog content and "purchase intent" (buy, purchase, discount, promo code,
  pricing, where to buy, near me, free shipping) to product/landing pages.

## Email / Newsletter (adapted from guidebook's 15 email prompts)

- **Promo:** write a promotional email for a 48-hour flash event on [PRODUCT].
- **Launch:** write a product-launch email creating excitement for the new
  [PRODUCT] line.
- **Educational:** write an educational email about the benefits of [TOPIC] a
  habit app that [VALUE].
- **Re-engagement:** write a re-engagement email offering [DISCOUNT] to
  subscribers inactive 6 months.
- **Testimonial/case study:** write an email showcasing this customer
  testimonial: [REVIEW].
- **Event invite:** write an email inviting subscribers to a free online
  workshop on [TOPIC] for [NICHE] looking to [DESIRED RESULTS].
- **Survey:** write an email requesting subscribers take a short survey about
  [TOPIC].

Rule (from the guidebook): emails should be genuine, engaging, targeted; treat
every ChatGPT output as a foundation to edit, not final copy.

## Quick reference — content intent mapping

| Intent | Content type | Example keyword |
|---|---|---|
| Curiosity / informational | Blog, guides, answers (Quora) | "how to build discipline" |
| Commercial | Comparisons, "vs", reviews | "habits garden vs habitica" |
| Transactional / purchase | Product + landing pages, promos | "habit tracker app download" |
| Local | Location pages (esp. Nigeria) | "sponsor a quest in [city]" |

## Verification note on prompts

Prompt output is a **draft, never final** — the Evidence Rule in SKILL.md still
applies: label any generated stat as `[ASSUMPTION]` unless you trace it to a
dated source, and verify claims against the real product before publishing.
